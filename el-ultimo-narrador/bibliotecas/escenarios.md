# 🌍 BIBLIOTECA DE ESCENARIOS (prompts reutilizables)

> Cada vez que generamos un escenario espectacular, lo guardamos aquí con su prompt. Con el tiempo tendrás cientos listos para reciclar → menos tokens, más consistencia visual.

`cinematic documentary, shallow depth of field, teal-and-orange grade, deep blacks, subtle film grain, volumetric light, 8k, hyper-detailed, prestige streaming documentary grade, 9:16 vertical`

## Escenarios semilla

### Laboratorio antiguo (s. XIX)
`ESC Laboratorio:` a dim 19th-century laboratory, wooden workbenches, brass instruments, glass beakers glowing faintly, sparks from a Tesla coil, dust in volumetric light beams, candle and early electric lamp mix + [base de estilo].

### Biblioteca antigua / archivo secreto
`ESC Biblioteca:` a vast candlelit ancient library, towering wooden shelves, leather tomes, floating dust, a single beam of light through a high window, warm gold tones + [base de estilo].

### Barco en tormenta (mar abierto)
`ESC Barco:` a wooden sailing ship battered by a violent storm at night, towering waves, lightning, rain, crew silhouettes gripping ropes, cold blue tones + [base de estilo].

### Selva perdida / ruinas
`ESC Selva:` dense misty jungle at dawn, ancient stone ruins swallowed by roots and vines, shafts of light through the canopy, fog, emerald and gold + [base de estilo].

### Calle antigua (medieval/renacentista)
`ESC Calle:` a narrow cobblestone medieval street at dusk, timber-framed houses, hanging lanterns, mist, a lone figure walking, warm amber light + [base de estilo].

### Desierto con estructura imposible
`ESC Desierto:` vast golden desert at golden hour, a mysterious ancient structure half-buried in sand, long shadows, heat haze, epic scale + [base de estilo].

### Cueva / ciudad subterránea
`ESC Subterraneo:` a massive underground carved-stone city, tunnels and chambers lit by torches, cold damp stone, deep shadows, sense of scale and mystery + [base de estilo].

### Documento / mapa antiguo (macro)
`ESC Documento:` extreme macro of an ancient parchment map, cracked ink, candlelight flicker, aged paper texture, shallow focus, gold and sepia + [base de estilo].

### Faro solitario (noche)
`ESC Faro:` a lone stone lighthouse on jagged rocks at night, beam cutting through fog, crashing waves, cold isolation, blue-grey palette + [base de estilo].

### Laboratorio/fábrica moderna
`ESC Fabrica:` an early 20th-century assembly line factory, rows of machines, workers in caps, steam and dust, warm industrial light + [base de estilo].
